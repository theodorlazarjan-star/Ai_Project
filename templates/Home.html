Welcome to the churn prediction

What is Customer Churn?

Customer churn means that a customer stops using a product or service, for example by cancelling their phone contract or subscription.

For companies, losing customers can mean losing future revenue. Churn prediction can help identify customers who are more likely to leave so that the company can take action before they churn.

What does this project do?

This project uses Machine Learning to predict whether a customer is likely to churn.

The model looks at different information about a customer, including their contract, services, payment method, monthly charges and how long they have been a customer.

Why is Churn Prediction useful?

  • Reduce customer loss
  • Improve customer satisfaction
  • Increase revenue
  • Make better business decisions
